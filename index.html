Do you only work with Shopify?

Shopify is our main platform for online stores. We also build WordPress and custom websites when a project needs it.

It depends on the number of products and custom sections. We give you a timeline with the quote, before any work starts.

Can I manage the store myself?

Yes. We build it so you can add products, change banners and edit pages without a developer, and we walk you through it at launch.

Do you work outside Kolkata?

Yes. We meet local clients in person and work with brands across India and abroad over video calls and chat.
